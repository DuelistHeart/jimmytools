# FAQ

## Does it send extra requests to the server?

No. The only commands it sends are the ones you trigger (`/skin`, from the transform key) or turn on
(`/names`, on join). The tab list and other displays only read data the server already sent.

## The custom tab list isn't showing

Make sure *Enable Custom Tablist* is on under *CustomTab* in the config, and that you are holding the Player List key.

## The tab list is cut off or too small

The scrolls fit themselves to the window. If they still look wrong, try a lower GUI scale in *Options → Video Settings*.

## Where is the config file?

`config/jimmytools.json5` inside your Minecraft folder. Delete it to reset to defaults.

## Something's broken

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/DuelistHeart/jimmytools/issues) with your `latest.log` attached.
